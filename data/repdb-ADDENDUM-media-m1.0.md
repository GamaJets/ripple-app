# RepDB — Media Use Addendum (m1.0)

**This addendum extends the RepDB Starter and Standard licenses with the right
to use the images and animation clips in the licensee's own published media —
videos, social posts, courses, presentations and thumbnails.**

## Applicability

RepDB offers this addendum to every licensee holding a Starter or Standard
license in version v1.1 or v1.2 for the order in question. It only grants; it
limits no right the license already gives, and the license is otherwise
unchanged.

The licensee accepts this addendum by making use of the rights granted below —
no notice to RepDB is required (§ 151 BGB). An order placed through a checkout
that names this addendum accepts it at purchase.

This addendum does not apply to the free tier or to the evaluation preview,
which have their own licenses.

## What it adds

The licensee may use the images and animation clips in media the licensee
publishes under its own brand — videos, social and short-form posts, courses
and lessons, presentations, ebooks, thumbnails and cover art, and marketing for
the licensee's own products — where the assets are incorporated into the
licensee's own work rather than published as the work itself.

This applies to free and to paid, monetised media alike.

## How the assets may appear

1. In any single frame, the RepDB assets together occupy **no more than 70 % of
   the frame area** — except for the full-frame moments allowed under 2. The
   area of an asset is the rectangle it is rendered into, not the opaque part
   of the picture: a transparent clip scaled to the full frame counts as the
   full frame.
2. Full-frame or near-full-frame moments are allowed for **no more than a third
   of the running time** of the piece, taken together.
3. In a still image, a page or a slide the assets sit inside the licensee's own
   layout and occupy no more than 70 % of it; the exception under 2 does not
   apply.
4. The licensee's own material accompanies them: own footage, a presenter,
   illustration, graphics, on-screen text, layout, or spoken narration and
   commentary. A plain or stock background, or a watermark, is not own
   material on its own.
5. A piece may not consist substantially of the assets played one after
   another.
6. Re-encoding, format conversion (for example to MP4 or GIF), cropping,
   resizing and colour adjustment are permitted, as they are under the license.
   None of them by itself makes an asset the licensee's own work.

## What it does not cover

- Media produced for a third party's brand — client work, agency deliveries and
  white-label media. Write to support@repdb.co for a separate agreement.
- Publishing an asset as the content itself: a clip posted as the video with
  nothing of the licensee's in it, or an image posted as the post, whether free
  or paid. That is redistribution of the raw asset in another wrapper and stays
  prohibited.
- Handing the assets on as assets — templates, presets, overlay or stock packs,
  or any download that lets others reuse them. The license's resale and
  bulk-provision rules apply unchanged.

## Everything else stays as it is

All other terms of the underlying license apply unchanged — in particular
resale and sublicensing, bulk provision of the dataset or image catalog, the
machine-learning and generative-model restrictions, attribution, term and
termination, warranty and liability, and governing law.
