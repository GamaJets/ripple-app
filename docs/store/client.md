### Description (≤ 4000)

Repple is the app your coach or gym gives you, so the plan they write is the one you train.

TRAIN YOUR PROGRAM
• Your coach's program, day by day: sets, reps, load, rest, supersets and notes.
• Log as you go. Tick a planned set, or type what you actually did. Logging still works without signal and sends once you're back online.
• A rest timer between sets, and a celebration when you set a new personal record.
• Exercise demos in the app, so you don't leave your session to look something up.

EAT TO YOUR TARGET
• Daily calories and macros set by your coach, or worked out from your own measurements.
• Log meals by search or barcode, and see what's left for the day.
• After an InBody scan, your calorie and macro targets update automatically from your new body composition. If your coach set them by hand, they get the new numbers to approve instead.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Weight, body fat, measurements and progress photos, with trends drawn only from real readings.
• InBody scan history, when your gym scans you.
• Streaks, badges and consistency, with an optional reminder before a streak breaks.

CONNECTED
• Import workouts from Apple Health, WHOOP and other connected devices.
• Message your coach, book sessions and classes, and check in each week.

You control every notification type in Settings → Notifications.

Repple needs an account from your coach or gym, or you can join with an invite code.

### What's new (1.3.0)

• A new dark design across the app.
• Workouts found on your watch now import in one tap from the Today screen.
• A haptic buzz when you set a personal record.
• Faster exercise lists, and bug fixes.
