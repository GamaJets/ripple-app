Reset your password

Enter the email address on your Repple account and we will send you a link to set a new password. The same account works across Repple, Repple Coach and Repple Studio, so one reset covers all three.

If it does not turn up. The mail can take a few minutes, and it very often lands in spam or junk — look there first.

On a work address, assume quarantine. Corporate filters — Microsoft 365 especially — accept the message and then hold it somewhere you are never shown, so there is nothing in your inbox and nothing in your spam folder. Ask your IT team to release it and to allow mail from repplefitness.com, or use a personal address instead.

Reset links work once and they expire, so if you have clicked an old one, ask for a fresh link rather than reloading it. Each new link cancels the one before it — requesting several in a row makes it likelier you open a dead one.

Still stuck? Email support@repplefitness.com from the address on the account and a person will sort it out. Support has more on emails that never arrive.
