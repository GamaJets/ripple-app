Payments

Where a trainer or gym takes payment through Repple, that agreement is between you and them, on their terms — including what happens about refunds and cancellations. Card processing is handled by Stripe. Subscriptions bought through the App Store are governed by Apple's terms and cancelled through your Apple account.

Repple is not invisible in that transaction, and it should not read as though it is. Where a sale goes through Repple's own checkout, the money lands in the seller's own Stripe account and Repple takes a platform fee of 10% of the sale, with Stripe's processing fee charged on top of that by Stripe. Nothing is taken from a payment a gym simply records at the desk, because no transaction passes through us. If a seller refunds a sale from inside Repple Coach, the 10% is returned with it; if they refund it from their own Stripe dashboard, it is not, because we are not party to that request. Pricing sets out all of this, including the parts that are still open.
