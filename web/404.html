Common guesses

Addresses people try,
and where the thing is.

Most of the traffic that reaches this page is a reasonable guess at a convention this site does not follow. Here are the ones worth answering rather than leaving you to try another.

Addresses people try, and where each one actually is
You triedWhere it actually is
/login, /signin, /sign-in There is no web sign-in. You sign in inside whichever app your role calls for. The only account jobs this site does are creating an account and resetting a password.
/app, /apps, /ios, /android /download — all three apps, and which one matches what you do.
/help, /faq /support — sorted by what has gone wrong rather than by feature.
/contact There is no contact form. One address reads everything: support@repplefitness.com.
/about The home page carries it — why Repple exists, written by the person the software kept failing.
/privacy-policy, /terms-of-service /privacy and /terms.
/coach with nothing after it That address does resolve — a coach's page carries their own handle on the end of it, and /coach says so when the handle is missing. Ask the coach for the link again rather than typing it.
/blog, /news, /careers, /press None of these exist. Repple has not launched publicly and there is nothing behind them to show you.

If your link was none of these, it is probably ours to fix. Send it to us and say where you found it.
