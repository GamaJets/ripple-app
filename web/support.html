Health, watches and devices

Do I have to allow Apple Health?

No — Repple works without it. Allowing it lets the app read your heart rate and calories during a session and import workouts you recorded on your watch, so you do not type them in twice. Change your mind any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Repple.

My Apple Watch workouts are not showing up.

Open the Train tab. If Repple can see workouts on your watch that are not yet in your log, it will say so and offer to bring them in. If nothing appears, check Apple Health is connected under Watch & Devices, and that the workout saved to the Health app on your iPhone.

Where is the heart-rate zone chart?

Zones need live heart-rate samples taken during the session, which in practice means an Apple Watch. Devices that only report a whole-day average — WHOOP and Oura among them — cannot produce a per-session breakdown, and Repple will not invent one from a daily figure. You get an explanation on screen instead of an empty chart.

Your log and your account

I logged a workout wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes. In the Train tab, find the entry under the day it was logged and tap the pencil beside it. You can change the exercise, the sets, the cardio figures and the calories.

I did not get a password reset email.

Ask for a new one at repplefitness.com/forgot-password and check your spam folder. Reset links can only be used once and do expire, so if you clicked an old one just request another. Still stuck after ten minutes? Email support@repplefitness.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me → Settings and request account deletion, or email support@repplefitness.com from the address on the account. See Privacy for exactly what happens to your data and when.
