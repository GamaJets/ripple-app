Questions people ask first

When will they be on the stores?

We are not going to give you a date we cannot keep. Both stores review submissions on their own timetable, and Google additionally requires a closed test to run for a set period before an app can go public. When each one is live this page gains a real download button and the sentence above disappears.

Do I need all three?

No. One account works across all three, but you only install the one for your role. A coach who also trains uses Repple Coach; a gym owner who coaches on the floor may want both.

Is there an Android version?

Yes — all three are built for Android as well as iPhone, and neither is behind the other. Both go to their stores together.

What does it cost?

Pricing is not published yet, and nothing on this site quotes one. Creating an account and logging your training costs nothing today. Email us and we will talk plainly about it rather than guessing in advance — Pricing sets out everything that can honestly be said in the meantime.

What happens to what I log before the apps are out?

It stays. The web account and the apps read the same database, so anything you record now is there when you sign in on a phone. See Privacy for what is stored and Deleting your account for how to remove it.
