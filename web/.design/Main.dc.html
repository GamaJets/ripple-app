Why Repple exists

Built by someone
the software kept failing.

Repple was not designed for gyms from the outside. All three apps came from being on the receiving end of software that did not work — as somebody training, as somebody being coached, and as somebody’s member.

Training

Meal plans in one app. Workouts in another. Measurements in a third. Progress somewhere else again.

Every one recorded something true, and not one could say what to do next — because none of them could see the others.

Being coached

Messaging a coach and waiting on a reply to find out whether a session could be booked.

And no shared view of progress underneath it — so every conversation started by re-establishing where we were against the goal we had already agreed.

Being a member

The gym I train at runs an app years past its best.

The people it fails hardest are the members it was bought for. An owner should be able to hand their clients something that works — and see what it tells them.

Three frustrations, one shape: the information existed, and nothing read it together. So Repple is one record with three ways in. It takes the whole picture — what you ate, what you lifted, what your body did about it — and says what to change. Everything else in this product is downstream of that.

The rule we build to

A number Repple cannot read shows a dash. Never a zero.

Advice is only worth having if the thing underneath it is real. An app that averages over nobody, or prices a month of sessions nobody marked, is not being helpful — it is guessing, in the same confident type it uses when it knows.

What one record makes possible
It reads everything together

Sleep, sessions, food, weight, body composition and how hard it felt — in one record, not five accounts that never speak.

It moves the targets for you

Calories and macros follow your actual body and your actual training, and change when those change. Not a number you set in January and stopped believing in February.

It says what to do next

Train, eat more, deload, sleep. One instruction, from the whole picture — and nothing at all on the days it genuinely does not know.
