The book

Every client, with the
context still attached.

Open a client and you get what they actually did — not what they told you they did. Their logged sessions, their loads, their check-ins, their measurements, their nutrition if they share it. The conversation starts from evidence.

  • Their real training history — the same log they keep, live, with no export step and no screenshots.
  • Programmes you write — built once, assigned out, landing directly in the client's Train tab.
  • Check-ins that surface drift — sleep, stress and soreness alongside the numbers, because the numbers rarely explain themselves.
  • One message thread per client — kept with their record, not lost in a phone.

The week

A schedule that knows
who is in front of you.

Sessions, classes and one-to-ones on a single timetable. Tap a booking and you are already in that client's record — no hunting, no second app, no trying to remember what you changed last time.

  • Bookings and attendance — mark who showed, so show rate is a measured figure rather than an impression.
  • Works with the gym — if you coach inside a Repple Studio gym, your delivered sessions feed their rota and payroll automatically.
  • Independent too — no gym required. Bring your own clients and Repple Coach is simply your book.

What it saves you

The admin you stopped
counting as work.

Nobody became a coach to retype numbers. These are the jobs Repple Coach takes off the pile.

Before

Chasing screenshots

Asking clients what they lifted, waiting, then transcribing it into your own sheet. Now: it is already there, because they logged it in the app you share.

Before

Rewriting the same block

Rebuilding a programme per client in a new document each time. Now: write it once, assign it out, adjust per person.

Before

Noticing too late

Realising in month three that someone stopped coming in week six. Now: they surface at the top of the list the week the pattern breaks.

One account, whatever you are

Coach at a gym on Tuesday,
train on your own on Thursday.

The same login opens Repple Coach for your client book and Repple for your own training. Your workouts are yours — a gym you coach at sees the sessions you deliver, not the sessions you do. And if you leave, your clients' records go with the clients, because they always belonged to them.
