The gym, run properly.

Repple is three apps sharing one backbone: one for the people who train, one for the people who coach them, and one for the people who have to keep the lights on.

Repple

Your programme, your meals, your progress and your bookings. Reads your Apple Watch so a session you have already done does not need typing in again.

For members

Repple Coach

Your clients, your schedule, your programme templates and your exercise library — with the analytics to see who is drifting before they leave.

For trainers

Repple Studio

The gym itself: trainers, classes, revenue and retention in one place, so the state of the business is something you can see rather than something you sense.

For gym owners

Every number comes from somewhere

Repple does not fill an empty screen with a plausible figure. If a measurement has not been taken, the app says so and asks for it. A number on screen can always be traced back to a row, a reading, or something you typed.
