The site moves, but the app has its own split to settle. A client is On track / Watch / At risk; a trainer is Healthy / Watch / Not delivering / Idle. Two scales sharing one middle word, inside one product — and the site’s “under-loaded” belongs to neither. Worth picking one scale before the site copies either. Not delivering is also a hard thing to print beside a person’s name on their employer’s dashboard.
