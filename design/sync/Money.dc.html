71% and 80% are both correct and both called fill. An owner comparing the class screen against the overview sees two numbers for one class and has no way to tell which is which. The library's split is the right one — booked against capacity is how full the room is; attended against booked is whether they showed — so the app's label is what changes, and the site keeps quoting the library.
