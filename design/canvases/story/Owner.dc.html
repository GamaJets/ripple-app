A gym runs six or seven systems that each work. The timetable knows who booked. The door knows who came. The card machine knows who paid. None of them know about each other — so every question that crosses two of them becomes an evening with a spreadsheet. And the app your members actually hold is usually the oldest thing in the stack.

What most gyms run today
Class booking system knows who booked
Door / access control knows who came
Card machine & direct debits knows who paid
A spreadsheet for PT commission rebuilt every month
A whiteboard for the rota photographed on Sundays
WhatsApp for everything else searchable by nobody

Each one is fine. The cost is not in any of them — it is in the reconciling, and it lands on one person, usually on a Sunday.
