Three apps · one record

The same complaint,
three scales up.

One person juggling five apps and a coach juggling forty clients are not different problems. Neither is a gym reconciling six systems on a Sunday. It is one problem at three sizes, which is why it is one record and three ways in — not three products that integrate.

Repple · for the person training
“Five apps, and none of them talk.”

Meals, sessions, measurements and progress in one place — with targets that move when your body does, and one instruction for today.

A day looks like

Log the session, photograph the meal, step on the scale on Sunday. The plan changes itself. If your coach ran the session, it is already there.

Repple Coach · for the coach
“Waiting on a reply to book a session.”

Availability a client can book against without asking. Programs, check-ins and progress in one place, so nobody starts a conversation by re-establishing where they are.

A day looks like

Log the session you just ran into their record. Hand a new client a six-character code. See which four people have not trained this week.

Repple Studio · for the gym
“Six systems, reconciled on Sundays.”

Timetable, door, memberships, payments, equipment and staff on one board — so you can ask which area is moving the others.

A day looks like

See who is in the building. Price payroll against sessions actually delivered — and nothing at all until every one has an outcome recorded.

A member logs a set. Their coach sees it that evening. The owner sees what it did to attendance, and to the month. Same row, same database, read three ways — by three people who each need something different from it.
